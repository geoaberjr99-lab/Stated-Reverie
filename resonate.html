ROOM II / RESONATED STATES

The Resonance of Time.

Every album has a story. Every story deserves its time.

Enter Resonated States

Resonated States grew from the relationship between music and memory. An album can become inseparable from a person, a place, a season, or a moment in time.

Resonated States gives the album a physical place in that story by bringing display and time together. The CD itself becomes part of the clock face while the album remains visible above it.

The intention is not simply to display music memorabilia. It is to create a personal object whose meaning changes with the person who owns it.
