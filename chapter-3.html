More Than the Object

Resonated States raised another question: what happens when the memory is not an album?

We keep photographs, notes, tickets, jewelry, small objects and countless things that may mean very little to anyone else. Preserving the object alone can leave the most important part behind—the reason it mattered.

The Memory Key Insert system grew from that realization.

The artifact needed a place. But so did the story. Who preserved it? What happened? Why does this moment still resonate?

The object could be protected. The meaning could be preserved with it.
