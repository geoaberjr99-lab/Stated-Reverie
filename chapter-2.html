The First Resonance

Music has an unusual relationship with memory. A few notes can return us to a person, a place, a season, or a moment we thought had disappeared.

That realization became the beginning of Resonated States.

The idea was not simply to display an album or build a clock. It was to bring music, memory and time together in one physical experience. The album remains visible. The CD becomes part of time itself. And the person who owns it supplies the meaning.

Resonated States became the first physical expression of that philosophy — the moment music, memory and time became an object.
