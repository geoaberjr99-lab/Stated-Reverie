HOUSE / OUR STORY

Design with purpose in mind.

We create physical places for the memories we don’t want to lose.

Enter the House

Stated Reverie begins with a simple observation: the objects people keep are often valuable for reasons that cannot be measured by the object itself.

A photograph, an album, a handwritten note, a ticket, or a small keepsake can carry an entire experience. Our work is about creating a considered place for those objects and, just as importantly, the stories attached to them.

Rather than designing storage for things, Stated Reverie explores ways to preserve context—why something mattered, who preserved it, and what should remain with it.
