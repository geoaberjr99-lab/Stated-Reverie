Building the House

Once individual memories had a place, another problem appeared: where do those preserved stories live together?

That question eventually led to the Reverie Vault — a place for preserved stories to live together.

The Archive was conceived as a growing environment for Memory Key Inserts—a way to organize and protect them without turning personal stories into anonymous storage.

That idea also helped define Stated Reverie itself. Resonated States was not an isolated idea. Memory Key Inserts were not another unrelated idea. The Reverie Vault was not simply a box.

They were becoming Rooms within the same House.

Different expressions. One philosophy.
