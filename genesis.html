ROOM IV / REVERIE VAULT — THE ARCHIVE

Reverie Vault: The Archive.

A personal gallery and vault for the stories that accumulate across a life.

Enter the Archive

The Reverie Vault is the larger archival environment developed to bring Memory Key Inserts together. The system combines a protective outer Gallery with removable Vault elements so individual memories can remain organized, accessible, and personal.

Its modular approach allows the archive to grow without turning meaningful objects into anonymous storage. Each piece can be removed, explored, and returned to its place.

The Reverie Vault remains in active physical development. The imagery shown on this site represents working prototypes while the system is refined for production.
